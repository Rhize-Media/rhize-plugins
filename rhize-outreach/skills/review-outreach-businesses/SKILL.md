# Review Outreach Businesses

Open the saved campaign's Businesses step. Each candidate must show matched business identity, website and GBP links, claimed-profile evidence, recent-review evidence, observed defects/opportunities, unknowns and source time. A source failure stays unknown.

Manual businesses enter through the same step and receive the same identity, website, geography, industry, suppression and conflict checks. The human may select, hold or reject. Selection starts deliverable preparation once and carries the exact candidate revision forward.
